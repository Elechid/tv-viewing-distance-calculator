What TV size fits a 2.5 m viewing distance?

For a 16:9 TV at 2.5 m, 65 inches gives about 32° of horizontal viewing angle, 75 inches about 37° and 83 inches about 40°. Consider 65 inches for a less immersive view or 75 or 83 inches for films. Measure eye-to-screen distance and check the bezel, stand and delivery route separately.
